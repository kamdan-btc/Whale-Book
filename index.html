Hour by hour today

—

Spot = Binance (USDT, USDC and FDUSD pairs) + OKX (all spot pairs). Perps = Binance (USDT, USDC and coin-margined) + OKX (all derivatives). Open interest adds Binance, Bybit and OKX. Imbalance = net market buying as a share of that market's volume, so spot and perps can be compared fairly. Open interest = total open perp positions: rising means new bets are being opened, falling means positions are closing. These reads describe who is driving price and how fragile the move is. They are not predictions. Use the "Next hr" column to see how often each read was followed by the move it hints at.

Track record of each read · since Jan 2024

—

Times = separate occasions (days in a row with the same read count once). Up 5 / Up 10 = how often BTC closed higher 5 or 10 sessions later (about one or two weeks). Med 10 = the typical move over those 10 sessions. Compare each row with "Any session": a read only means something if it beats that. The samples are small, so treat a read as a lean, not a promise.

Recent sessions

—

A session is one US trading day. Funds report after the US close (9pm UK), so a day's figure usually lands overnight and shows here the next morning UK time. Flows are in US dollars: creations minus redemptions across all US spot Bitcoin ETFs. BTC price is the reference price at the US close. The reads look at the last 5 sessions together, because single days are noisy. Flows mostly follow price, so on their own they rarely call turns: the reads that held up best are the ones where flows and price disagree.

Data: The Commoner Bitcoin ETF Tracker by TFTC, licensed CC BY 4.0. Reads and statistics are worked out on this page from that data.

Sizes are resting orders at one price level on one venue, in coins. Perp books (Binance, Bybit and Bitfinex perps) are where most leverage sits. Bitfinex levels are grouped to 4 significant figures (for example $10 steps on BTC) and Hyperliquid levels to wider steps ($100 on BTC), because those exchanges only share a limited number of levels for free. ● ×N means a wall has stayed within 0.05% of the same price for N refreshes in a row; walls that vanish after one refresh are often spoofs. Data comes straight from each exchange's public API; a venue shows "blocked" if your network or region refuses it.
